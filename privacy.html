Privacy policy

What happens to your data when you visit this site or contact me. Written to be read, not skimmed past.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

The short version

No cookies. No analytics. No advertising or tracking. The site doesn't store anything about you. I only receive the information you decide to send me by email or when you book a call, and I use it only to answer you and work with you.

Who is responsible for your data

Cherif Aboubacar Camara (Abou Camara), independent consultant, Fès, Morocco. Contact: aboucamara1107@gmail.com.

This policy follows Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of personal data and, for visitors in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What is collected, and why

1. When you browse the site

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data needed to deliver the pages and protect the site against attacks: your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. This is based on a legitimate interest in keeping the site available and secure.

The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. To send them, Google's servers receive your IP address. No cookie is set. This is based on a legitimate interest in displaying the site correctly.

2. When you use the contact form

The form doesn't send anything to a server. When you click the button, it simply opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing leaves your device until you press "send" yourself.

If you send it, I receive your name, email address, project type, budget range and message in my inbox (Gmail, run by Google). I use them only to reply to you and, if you ask for one, to prepare a proposal. The legal basis is the steps taken at your request before a possible contract.

3. When you email me directly

Same as above: I receive what you write and your email address, and I use them to answer you.

4. When you book a call

Bookings go through Cal.com, which collects your name, email address, the time slot and any notes you add, and passes them on to me. Cal.com's own privacy policy applies to their service.

5. If we work together

I keep the details needed to run the project and to invoice it: name, company, contact details, the agreed proposal and invoices.

How long it is kept

  • Messages that don't lead to a project: deleted at the latest 2 years after our last exchange.
  • Client records and invoices: kept as long as accounting and tax law requires, up to 10 years.
  • Hosting logs: kept by Cloudflare for a short period, under their own policy.

Who else sees it

Your data is never sold, rented or shared for marketing. The only services involved are the ones needed to run the site and answer you:

  • Cloudflare (hosting and security)
  • Google (Gmail for email, Google Fonts for the fonts)
  • Cal.com (call booking)

Some of these companies are based in the United States, so your data may be processed outside Morocco and the European Union. They rely on recognised safeguards for these transfers, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see the data I hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit how it is used, object to its use, or get a copy of it. Just write to aboucamara1107@gmail.com. I'll reply within one month.

If you think your data isn't being handled properly, you can file a complaint with the CNDP in Morocco (cndp.ma) or with the data protection authority of your country if you live in the European Union.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS only, has no database and no user accounts, and uses strict security headers that block unauthorised scripts. Found a security problem? Please report it privately to aboucamara1107@gmail.com.

Children

This site is aimed at professionals and students aged 16 and over. I don't knowingly collect data about younger children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
