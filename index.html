About

The person behind the work

I'm Abou Camara — an independent consultant running my own practice from Fès, Morocco, working with founders and small teams across Europe and West Africa.

I come from a Big Data engineering background, which means I can actually build what I recommend. But engineering alone was never the point. What I care about is the full chain: understanding who buys, why they buy, and building the system that turns that understanding into revenue.

That's the gap I fill. Marketing people can't build. Developers can't sell. I do both — I'll write the positioning, design the funnel, code the automation that runs it, and hand you something that works without me. My own tools are proof of concept: a lead engine that qualifies prospects on its own, a platform that guides students to the right path, a dashboard that turns raw data into decisions.

The sales half isn't improvised either. I'm working through a structured remote-sales and copywriting programme — qualification, discovery, objection handling — because the gap between a good tool and a sold tool is a skill you learn, not a talent you have.

I work directly, without jargon, with a progress update every week. You always know where your project stands.
