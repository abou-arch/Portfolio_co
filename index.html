What I do

Four disciplines. One system.

In small businesses, things tend to get stuck between people. The marketing plan never turns into a tool, or the website never gets connected to how sales happen. I work on all four parts, so I can see where those gaps are and close them myself.

01

Consulting & Strategy

Before building anything, we work out who you're selling to and what should come first.

  • Audit & recommendations
  • Positioning & offer clarification
  • Roadmap and priorities
02

Sales Systems

A way to collect leads and sort them, so you call the serious ones first instead of going down the list in order.

  • Lead capture & qualification
  • Funnels & automated follow-up
  • CRM or Sheets pipeline tracking
03

Development & Automation

Websites and tools that stay easy to maintain, and automations for the repetitive work that eats your week.

  • Websites & web applications
  • API integrations & workflows
  • Data pipelines & AI-assisted tasks
04

Product Design

Screens people get on the first try, on a phone as much as on a laptop.

  • Visual identity & art direction
  • UI/UX mockups & prototypes
  • Design systems & redesigns

Why all four?

Because they depend on each other. The strategy decides what the sales system needs to capture, the build makes it work, and the design is what gets people to use it. When one person handles all of it, less gets lost along the way.

About

The person behind the work.

I like working on both sides: understanding the customer, and building the tool.

I'm Abou Camara. I work on my own from Fès, Morocco, with founders and small teams in Europe and West Africa. If we work together, you deal with me directly, from the first call to the handover.

I studied Big Data engineering, so I can build what I recommend. Over time I noticed that the code was rarely what held a project back. More often, the people who understood the customers and the people building the tools just weren't talking to each other.

So that's how I work. I write the positioning, design the funnel, code the automation behind it, and document it so you can run it without me. I'm also still learning the sales side properly, through a structured remote-sales and copywriting programme.

What I want to build are systems a small business can run day to day, that don't promise more than they do, and that save people time.

How I work

From the problem to the system.

Writing code is one step out of five. I start by making sure I understand the problem, and I stick around after launch.

  1. 01

    Understand

    A 30-minute call where I mostly ask questions. You'll leave with two or three things you can act on, whether or not we end up working together.

  2. 02

    Strategise

    Within 48 hours you get a written proposal: scope, approach, timeline, deliverables and a fixed price.

  3. 03

    Build

    I build in short iterations and send you an update every week. You can look at the work in progress whenever you like.

  4. 04

    Automate

    I automate the repetitive parts and connect everything to the tools you already use, so nobody has to sort things by hand.

  5. 05

    Improve

    Launch, handover of all access and documentation, and 30 days of support. After that, we improve things based on how people use it.
