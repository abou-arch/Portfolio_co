01Who it’s for

For products that work but feel hard to use.

Maybe people get lost halfway through a form, or the site looks different on every page. Or it's a new project that needs an identity and a clear interface from the start.

02What I do

From the logo to the last button.

  • Visual identity: logo, colours, typography and how they're used.
  • Interface design: mockups and prototypes for web and mobile.
  • Design systems: reusable components and simple rules, so new pages stay consistent.
  • Redesigns of existing interfaces that have grown confusing.
  • Accessibility: readable text, enough contrast, keyboard navigation.

03How it goes

Start with the task, not the look.

We begin with what people come to do and where they get stuck. Then layout, then visual design, then the real thing, tested on actual phones. Because I also build, the design is checked against what the code can do as we go.
