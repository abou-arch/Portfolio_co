01Who it’s for

For teams that need a tool, or lose hours to manual work.

You need a website or an internal tool that does one job well. Or your team copies data between apps every week, and you suspect a machine could do it.

02What I do

From a landing page to a small application.

  • Websites and landing pages that load fast and work on phones.
  • Web applications: questionnaires, dashboards, admin screens, ordering flows.
  • Integrations and automations between the apps you use, with Make, webhooks and APIs.
  • Data work in Python: cleaning, analysis and dashboards.
  • AI-assisted tasks, where they actually save time.

03How it goes

Small steps, visible progress.

I build in short iterations and send you an update every week. You can look at the work in progress whenever you like. At the end you get the code, every access, the documentation, and 30 days of support.

04Choosing the tool

Sometimes the right answer is no code.

For a quick test or a tool a non-technical owner will maintain, a visual automation in Make is often the better choice. When volume, complexity or testing needs grow, it's time to move to code. I'll tell you which side of that line you're on, and why.
